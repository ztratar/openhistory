# Code of conduct

OpenHistory contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and constructive.

- Treat people with empathy and respect across issues, pull requests, reviews, and community spaces.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person who contributed them.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about another person.
- Handle reports involving sensitive activity data discreetly. Never ask someone to post raw personal history publicly.
- Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact or GitHub private vulnerability reporting. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and respond proportionately.
